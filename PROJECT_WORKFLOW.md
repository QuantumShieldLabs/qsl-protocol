# QSL project workflow

Goals: G1, G2, G3, G4, G5

Revision: 2026-10-16. Operator-adopted project workflow; bounded AUTO and mandatory final review retained. Operator-authorized preparation improvements apply prospectively at a verified quiet boundary.

## Authority and scope

This is the single project-wide build method for the Director, Codex and any other assistant, across protocol, desktop, relay, attachments, website, design and supporting tools. Current human instructions govern. Accepted product contracts and requirements still define what must be built. This document defines how that work is scheduled and assessed; it supersedes earlier procedural rules, autopilot policies, standing-rule ceremonies and assistant workflows. Historical decisions keep their product meaning and evidence, but their old process requirements do not become current instructions.

Use one active task record maintained by the Director. In the managed build it is the current lane TASK named by the build-root instructions. Repository queue files are compatibility views and historical indexes, never independent launch authority. The roadmap holds product order; HARDENING.md holds deferred rigor. Neither starts work by itself. Copies of this workflow in component repositories must match the canonical build copy; repository instructions may add relevant technical constraints, not a competing method.

## Current operating mode

Bounded AUTO, explicitly authorized by the operator on October 14, 2026. At most one issued/launchable task and one Codex executor at a time; zero is valid at a quiet boundary. The first prepared vault ownership task is manually launched by the operator. After it starts, the Director checks for a result every 15 minutes using the existing thread heartbeat; after verifying each exact result, source/evidence and executor/owned-child exit, the Director may prepare, finally review and automatically launch one fresh bounded successor. Ordinary workers cannot launch successors. A result or stop never restarts a consumed attempt. There are no automatic retries of consumed tasks, probes, CI jobs or unknown-effect operations, no parallel agents and no additional schedules. Stop affected work for unresolved security/design/authority/integrity decisions, denied actions or unknown effects; retain an honest blocked record. Human merges/deploys and privileged maintenance remain separate. Read-only CI observation is not a retry; existing maintenance is not launch authority. Later human pause or mode changes override this authorization.

Director: GPT 6.1 Sol / High. Codex: GPT 6.1 Sol / Medium. Normal speed; no /fast. Fresh executor sessions, memories off and subagents off. Pushover is USER_PAUSED: no enqueue, send, flush or retry. Resume or mode/model changes require a later human instruction. Tool capabilities or old configuration defaults do not supply that instruction.

## Lane 1: connected working slices

Keep the accepted roadmap. Choose the smallest coherent change that advances an actual product behavior or removes a demonstrated blocker. A slice includes its real caller, necessary error/refusal behavior and affected fixtures. Avoid frameworks, general tooling, speculative future cases and repeated broad reviews unless a concrete requirement or failure justifies them.

Prepare tasks around complete behavior, not the number of files, warnings or commands. Keep a connected change, its actual caller, necessary fixtures, success/refusal paths and immediate checks in one realistic assignment. Independent sensitive review remains separate from the author. Split work when contracts, dependencies or the measured budget require it; do not split a settled correction into one task per compiler warning.

When required build checks repeatedly stop at the next target's warning, collect the reachable diagnostics across the required configurations in one prospectively scoped, bounded inventory before preparing another repair. Use a documented keep-going option where appropriate, retain incomplete/dependent-target coverage honestly, and settle the actual causes and equivalence before authorizing a bounded related batch. This grants no native command, arbitrary lint cleanup, waiver, target reduction or retry. Original required quality commands must still pass after the corrections.

Keep the worker-facing packet concise: lead with the outcome, exact allowed change, first actual-caller check, required final evidence and stopping conditions. State each current numerical requirement once in its authoritative contract or plan. Link exact preserved evidence and the current rules instead of copying historical counts, old commands or whole reports into the directive, TASK and contract. The complete final scope/input list, hashes, permissions and budget remain available and must agree; concise instructions do not omit safety or weaken the mandatory final review.

Reuse the last verified finite test driver and result interpretation for the same output format and fault model. Review only the necessary adapter differences. Before the first product command, exercise an adapted interpreter on preserved representative raw outputs: a pass, its intended failure, no tests or an unexpected result, and the actual event/child shape when used. The check must distinguish those outcomes without rerunning a product subject or broadening a causal predicate. Preserve exact tool versions, fixtures and results in the existing preparation/task evidence. A later interpretation defect may be corrected by a preserved read-only interpretation of the original result; it never justifies rerunning a consumed subject. Do not build a new general runner or another review framework for this precheck.

Before issue, the Director names the roadmap card, user-visible purpose, exact source base and allowed paths, settled contracts, immediate checks, necessary independent review, deferred checks with ledger IDs, time limit, closeout reserve and exit condition. Read the full relevant current instructions, contracts and source; retrieve exact cited evidence before making a finding. Do not require the whole historical archive at every startup. Resolve structural identity, format, authentication, ownership and capacity interfaces before dependent code relies on them; a mock cannot quietly choose an unresolved production contract.

Implement in an isolated branch/worktree, preserve unrelated work, run the smallest meaningful changed-boundary checks, review the complete changed sensitive interface and record the result. For a bug, use a real caller regression that distinguishes the defect from setup failure. Add tests for new behavior and risk, not tests that merely mirror a low-impact prose edit. Reuse unchanged evidence when its exact source/configuration and claim scope remain valid. CI and branch-protection checks remain enforced; do not disable them or manufacture green results. After every push, inspect all triggered CI checks through completion for the exact published head. Fix every failure and verify the corrected commit passes; do not ignore a failure or label it pre-existing to declare the work finished. Keep required checks enforced, retain original failure evidence and distinguish a source correction with fresh CI from rerunning a consumed job. If an external condition prevents repair or verification, state the exact blocker and keep completion/merge withheld rather than report a pass.

Put confirmed changes in the relevant source repository and push the reviewed branch after its applicable validation and publication checks. Open a reviewable PR when appropriate. Local candidates, reports and branch pushes are separate facts; a push is not a merge. The human operator alone merges, deploys and authorizes privileged host changes. Preserve published history; no force push, destructive reset or silent loss of dirty work. Use merge commits under existing repository policy.

## Mandatory final assignment review

Before marking any new or revised assignment READY, giving its manual launch command or automatically launching it, the Director must reread the complete final saved directive from beginning to end, together with its actual saved scope/input list and launch command. Review the version Codex will receive after all edits, not an earlier draft, recalled wording or a checklist alone. Apply this to implementation, review, design and supporting tasks.

Check the assignment from Codex's point of view:

1. **One useful outcome:** name one demonstrable product behavior or one specific blocker resolved, its roadmap position and honest target stage. Split independent outcomes before issue; keep a connected safety boundary complete.
2. **Complete, bounded scope:** include necessary real callers, fixtures and success/error/refusal/recovery/ownership behavior; verify allowed paths/effects and settled contracts. Required safety, independent sensitive review, numerical limits and applicable CI remain intact. Identify unresolved dependencies before dependent use.
3. **Realistic budget:** account for source complexity, reading, compilation, debugging, meaningful tests and closeout reserve using available measurements. If the work cannot credibly fit, revise its scope or sequence before READY. Preserve required atomic/accepted interfaces; obtain a real contract decision when changing them is necessary. Do not compensate by cutting safety or promising completion without evidence.
4. **Clear first action and priorities:** Codex can identify what to implement and the earliest actual-caller check. Essential supporting work has a named product need and finite finish line. Avoid burying implementation under long historical reads, machinery or paperwork.
5. **Decisive acceptance and stopping criteria:** distinguish implemented source, verified caller behavior, independent review, full qualification, publication/CI and release. Require meaningful positive/refused controls, actual results/exits and an honest closeout if unfinished; a completion report alone is not acceptance.
6. **Remove unnecessary work:** eliminate duplicate records, speculative cases, universal apparatus promises, broad unchanged reviews and ceremony. Put genuinely deferred rigor in the single HARDENING ledger with cut/risk/earliest mandatory trigger, without moving a reached gate or excusing a known safety defect.
7. **Consistent final packet:** saved directive, scope, source/input pins, mode/model, permissions, budget, command and current TASK agree. The current max1/normal mode and recorded launcher authority, memories/subagents off and Pushover USER_PAUSED must agree with the latest human instruction. Never amend a running/consumed packet to satisfy this review.

Correct a failed item before issue, then reread the final saved packet again after changes. Record one short final-review disposition and the reviewed directive/scope identities or exact manifest reference in the existing TASK or preparation record. Do not create a second queue, separate review task, new approval ceremony or enforcement framework. Launcher/checksum/scanner validation and the Director's judgment about scope and wording are separate required checks; neither establishes the other. This pre-issue review is the Director's responsibility and does not replace the independent sensitive implementation review.

## Earlier checks and useful progress

When changing workflow documentation, separate procedural instructions from product contracts before editing. Preserve supported behavior, authorization rules, durability/recovery limits and unsupported cases in a discoverable technical entry point. Read the complete affected guidance and run existing relevant documentation/contract checks before publication. If moving a technical statement, retain its meaning and update affected references and contract checks coherently; do not remove the statement or weaken its check to obtain a pass. This applies to both Director and Codex.

For each product slice, exercise the actual user or application entry point early, using synthetic data where required. Check its meaningful success and refusal paths before investing in supporting machinery. For the vault/profile slice, this means create or initialize, open, save, reopen and recover/refuse as the accepted contract requires, through the real callers. Helper/model tests support that evidence; they do not replace it. For design or supporting work that cannot exercise a product entry point, name the exact product blocker, the evidence this task can establish and its finite exit condition. Do not imply an end-to-end product pass from a helper demonstration.

At every failure or review finding, state whether the evidence shows a reachable product defect, missing acceptance evidence, a test/apparatus limitation, or a combination. Record the exact source, observed cause, affected requirement and claim that remains unsupported in the existing task/report. A symbolic or synthetic scenario is not proof of production reachability. Unknown reachability remains unknown; reproduce through the real boundary or explicitly bound the uncertainty. Fix demonstrated defects affecting the slice immediately. Schedule only legitimately deferrable proof or apparatus work in HARDENING.md, with its risk and earliest mandatory trigger. Classification cannot waive the safety core, required CI or a triggered gate.

Judge progress at task closeout by the behavior that now works through actual callers, meaningful test/review results, the exact code changes and where they are saved/published, completed CI on the published head, and remaining ledger obligations. Investigations and documentation improvements count as the blocker or decision they resolve; they do not count as implemented app features. Use the existing TASK, report, PR and hardening ledger for these facts. Avoid new dashboards, parallel records or another review stage solely to measure progress. Before issuing another task for a recurring problem, explain what the previous task established and why the next scope changes the cause or supplies the missing decisive evidence.

## The safety core is due now

Never defer correctness needed by the behavior being implemented: authentication before trust, secure key/randomness handling, nonce ownership, strict bounded untrusted input, safe vault writes/recovery, commit before external release, exact retry of committed operations, fail-closed critical errors and truthful user-visible security/delivery state. A known violation blocks the affected behavior. Missing acceptance evidence is recorded as missing, never PASS.

Changed cryptography, authentication, keys, persisted security formats, ownership/durability boundaries and their security assertions need independent sensitive review before dependent work uses the changed foundation. Author self-review does not satisfy it. Keep accepted interfaces and numerical requirements; this method does not lower workloads, increase caps, introduce fallback or approve a new trust assumption.

Broader assurance can be staged: exhaustive stress/soak, whole-platform matrices, formal proof expansion, release packaging, external audit preparation and stronger optional test-apparatus guarantees. Defer only if the current slice remains safe within a stated development context and the omitted proof is not claimed. Required CI continues to run. A known reachable security defect cannot be relabelled deferred qualification to cross a safety gate.

## Lane 2: one hardening ledger

HARDENING.md is the one ledger for every shortcut as it is taken. Use a stable ID and exactly three substantive fields: **Cut/deferred**, **Risk**, **Mandatory trigger**. The first field includes the exact omitted work, affected requirement/source and responsible roadmap card. The trigger names an observable event and the checks/review that must be completed before crossing it; "later", "when time permits" and "before the end" are invalid.

The ledger has no active executor, launch command or separate queue. At issue, review and any proposed milestone, merge, data-use, external-review, deployment or release boundary, the Director checks which triggers are reached. A triggered item is brought into the same single active task queue; the triggering boundary stays blocked until its required evidence is accepted. A trigger can be earlier than release. If a deferral depends on synthetic data, enforce that restriction until its real-data trigger is closed. Revisions to a trigger record authority and reason; do not move it silently to escape a gate.

Retain closed entries with an evidence reference in their fields. Existing owed records and historical findings are source inventories, not additional active hardening ledgers. Import actual applicable debt without assuming historical "open" means a current defect or historical "closed" proves current correctness. Unresolved legacy findings must be reconciled before the affected foundation is used, its external review, or its release claim. Do not forget them or restart an entire historical audit before unrelated coding.

## Progress and acceptance stages

**IMPLEMENTED** means source exists; decisive checks may still be missing. **SLICE_VERIFIED** means the stated development behavior works through actual callers, immediate tests pass and needed sensitive review is accepted. **CORE_READY** means its safety-critical contracts and tests support the exact dependent use; remaining broader qualification is named in the ledger. **QUALIFIED** means the complete accepted card/milestone obligations, including triggered debt, are satisfied. **RELEASE_READY** additionally requires the final supported-platform, formal, external-review, service, packaging and human gates applicable to the intended release.

Dependent candidate development may proceed on a specifically reviewed CORE_READY foundation without waiting for unrelated final qualification. It may not rely on an unsettled contract, unsafe foundation or unproved bound needed for that use. Existing "ACCEPTED" card exits mean QUALIFIED unless explicitly recorded as a narrower investigation or design acceptance. A demonstration, test exit, report, PR, merge and release are never interchangeable.

## Finite tasks and honest recovery

Design supporting tools for the actual workload and stated fault model. Use an adequate bounded supervisor for potentially nonterminating administrative checks, trustworthy subject exits, source/artifact identity, raw evidence custody and privacy. Do not demand universal corruption resistance or arbitrary exception-graph accounting as a prerequisite to product coding unless that guarantee is actually required and justified. Missing stronger apparatus proof remains unclaimed and enters the ledger if deferred. Changing an approved runtime method's guarantee requires an explicit recorded assessment; this workflow is not permission to silently alter an issued probe.

An understood, reversible in-scope preparation or implementation defect may be corrected within the same task and budget, with original failure evidence retained and affected checks rerun. No automatic retry of a consumed task, probe, CI job or unknown-effect operation. A terminal stop returns to the Director: recover the result, identify the cause, propose a corrected scope or method, and use the current operator-authorized launch mode for a fresh scoped task if one is needed. Repeated failures of the same method require a whole-method assessment, not another symptom-only assignment. Unknown effects, lost integrity, unscoped/privileged acts and genuine design decisions stop affected work before the action.

Leave time to save the report and establish process completion. Confirm the exact executor/session and owned administrative children have exited; report unknown numeric exit codes as unknown. Never clean up by a broad process-name kill. Preserve issued inputs, failures, receipts and sealed evidence; corrections are new records. Install prospective instruction changes only at a verified quiet boundary. Do not rewrite old input manifests or reseal old reports to fit new rules.

## One durable record and one explanation

At meaningful boundaries update the single TASK with authority, phase/stage, exact branch/source, last verified result/evidence, unresolved findings, ledger IDs and next authorized action. The task report carries its relevant proof; references to exact immutable artifacts are adequate. Do not copy entire histories into each directive or stop, mandate duplicate journal/relay/prediction records, split routine work into promotion/implementation/closeout PRs, or require a new approval for an already authorized routine act.

When issuing or receiving work, explain in plain language: which part of the plan, what problem, what changed or will change, evidence and limits, and the next concrete step. Completion notices are reports awaiting verification. Never promise a finish date without measured support.

Keep credentials, keys, private raw logs and host topology out of public repositories/chat. Preserve existing secret/publication scanners, scope guards, hooks and meaningful CI. Use the configured GitHub remote, verify publication identity, and state where each artifact is actually saved. A local file or platter copy is not off-host durability. Recovery must restore this workflow, the single TASK and HARDENING.md together, then reconcile live source and consumed tasks before resuming.
