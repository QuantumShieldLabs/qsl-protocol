Domain reviews are advisory and scoped to one concrete decision or sensitive changed interface. The Director owns disposition and the single task record; reviewers do not issue tasks, create queues, merge or authorize runtime/privileged/public acts. Current one-task mode does not allow parallel agents. A required independent review is separate from authorship and precedes dependent use.
